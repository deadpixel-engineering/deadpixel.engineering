## From Naples to New York: The Global Pizza Journey

Pizza, that beloved dish of **dough**, _sauce_, and toppings, has evolved into countless regional variations. Let's explore some of the most distinctive styles!

### 1. Neapolitan Pizza

The original pizza from Naples follows strict [traditional guidelines](https://www.pizzanapoletana.org/en/):

- Must use San Marzano tomatoes
- Requires buffalo mozzarella
- Cooked at 900°F (485°C)
- **_Never_** longer than 90 seconds

### 2. New York Style

> "It's all about the fold"
> -- _Every New Yorker ever_

The defining characteristics include:

1. Large, wide slices
2. Thin, crispy crust
3. Eaten folded in half
   - Creates structural integrity
   - Controls oil drip
   - Maximum mobility

### 3. Chicago Deep Dish

~~Just a casserole masquerading as pizza~~

**Actually** a legitimate style featuring:

- [x] Deep walls of crust
- [x] Chunky tomato sauce
- [x] Layers of cheese
- [ ] Ability to eat without fork and knife

## Pizza Making Tips

Here's a simple sauce recipe:

```
1 can whole peeled tomatoes
2 cloves garlic, minced
1 tbsp olive oil
Salt to taste
```
